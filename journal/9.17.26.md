So chapter 2 was labelled "The Psychology of Everyday Actions" and there was quite a bit of topics that Don Norman jumped between. 
To summarize, I think the chapter talked about how good design requires the designer to be able to understand how people will think, act and respond when it comes to using a product.
We talked about the gulf of execution which is the bridge between what a person is trying to do and them figuring out how to do it. On the other hand, we have the gulf of evaluation which is
the bridge between performing an action and registering whether it did the desired result or not. So the consensus between these two gulfs is that good design must cross the bridge that is the both of these gulfs
in terms of signifiers, feedback and the topics we talked about earlier in Chapter 1
